01 / About

Operations first, then the data behind them.

I started on the floor. At Amazon I scanned, sorted and shipped at fulfilment-centre speed. At Ballsportdirekt.de (11teamsports Group) I spent three and a half years booking goods receipts and stock movements in ZeyOS ERP/WMS, running cycle counts, clearing discrepancies — and eventually onboarding and training the team.

Today, as Business Operations Coordinator at Ramro Imports B.V., I work on the other side of the chain: coordinating producers in Nepal, international shipments to the Netherlands and B2B orders across Europe — prices, quantities, quality, documents and delivery dates.

Along the way I learned that most supply-chain problems show up in the data first. So I trained in SAP S/4HANA (Sourcing & Procurement, MM) and build reporting in Excel and Power BI to catch them early. My M.Sc. in International Management & Psychology is complete apart from the thesis.
