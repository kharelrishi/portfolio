Step 1 · Source

One Excel sheet, and three problems

What needed fixing

  • Two rows before the dataA title row and a header row sat above the first sale.
  • Every column read as textNumbers couldn't be summed until they were typed.
  • One broken cellA stray character in Volume turned that row's Revenue into an Excel error.
Step 2 · Prepare

Seven Power Query steps turn the sheet into a table

#StepWhat it does
1Source & navigationOpens Sheet1 of the workbook
2Skipped rowsDrops the title row and the header row
3Renamed columnsColumn1–9 become Year, Channel … Revenue
4Valid rowsKeeps only rows whose Year is a real number
5Cleared source errorsTurns Excel error cells into blanks

Result: 15,118 rows load with zero refresh errors.

KPI tiles that adapt to the filters

Revenue Trend Label =
VAR yoy  = [Revenue YoY %]
VAR cagr = [Revenue CAGR %]
RETURN
SWITCH( TRUE(),
  NOT ISBLANK( yoy ),
    FORMAT( yoy, "▲ +0.0%;▼ -0.0%" ) & " vs " & ( year - 1 ),
  NOT ISBLANK( cagr ),
    FORMAT( cagr, … ) & " CAGR …",
  "First year in range"
)

All years selected → ▲ +5.3% CAGR 2011–2014. Only 2014 → ▼ −1.3% vs 2013. A matching colour measure turns the text green or red, and the arrow says the same thing in words. (Code shortened.)

Step 6 · Design

One theme and one grid on every page

Every page follows the same layout: a navy header with the title and page buttons, a filter bar (Year, Channel, Product Group, Producer), a row of KPI tiles, then charts and tables. Filters are synced, so a selection follows you from page to page.

Navy · headers Deep blue · main series Teal · profit & growth Amber · margin lines Coral · decline & losses

Problems hit, and how they were solved

  • Numbers loaded as textTyped every column in Power Query.
  • Measures lost after a saveRecreated them and saved as a Power BI project (PBIP).
  • One error cell broke the refreshError cells become blanks and are flagged.
  • Building visuals by hand was slowGenerated pages from a script and a theme file.
  • Page buttons did not renderReplaced them with a built-in page navigator.
